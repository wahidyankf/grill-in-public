# Governance Principles

These principles are the foundation of `repo-governance/`. All governance
policies, workflows, and future documents must follow them. When a conflict is
found, resolve it before adopting the lower-level guidance; do not leave
contradictory rules in place.

## Principles

- [Progressive Disclosure](progressive-disclosure.md) — keep guidance focused
  and load only what the task needs.
- [Root Cause Orientation](root-cause-orientation.md) — fix the responsible
  cause rather than suppressing its symptoms.
